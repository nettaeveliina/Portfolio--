A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Netta.

Web Developer & UI/UX Designer

I am a Business Information Technology graduate (BBA) based in Oulu, Finland. My skills focus on web development, frontend development and UI/UX design.

I enjoy combining technology, visual design and practical problem-solving to create digital solutions that are clear, accessible and easy to use.

Through personal and independent projects, I have gained hands-on experience in designing and developing websites and browser-based applications for real-world use cases. My work has included responsive frontend development, UI/UX design, database integration, search engine optimization and content development.

I have also designed and developed a browser-based business management application featuring CRM, customer management, work orders, scheduling, quotations, invoicing, time tracking, product management and role-based user access.

I am particularly interested in opportunities in web development, frontend development and UI/UX design where I can combine technical implementation with user-centered design.
